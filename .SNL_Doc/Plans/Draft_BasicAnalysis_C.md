# Draft — Basic Analysis, Part C: Differentiation and Integration

Phase 1 drafting blueprint for the "Differentiation and Integration" portion of the SNL
`Basic_Analysis` library (Fulcrum-Notes-SNL). Scope:

1. **Differentiation of one real variable** — derivative, differential, arithmetic / chain / inverse
   rules, elementary derivatives, higher derivatives, convexity via the second derivative.
2. **Mean value theorems** — Fermat, Rolle, Lagrange, Cauchy, L'Hôpital, Taylor's formula with Peano
   and Lagrange remainders, standard equivalent infinitesimals.
3. **Antiderivatives / indefinite integrals** — primitive, substitution, integration by parts,
   integration of rational functions.
4. **Riemann definite integral** — partitions and Riemann sums, Riemann integrability, Darboux
   upper/lower sums and Darboux's theorem, integrability criteria (uniform continuity, monotone;
   Lebesgue criterion is only stated), mean value theorems for integrals, Newton–Leibniz formula,
   improper integrals. **Lebesgue integration, measurable functions and L^p spaces are out of scope
   (covered by the `measure-theory` and `real-analysis` libraries); the Lebesgue integrability
   criterion appears as a single theorem statement, with its proof deferred to measure theory.**
5. **Sequences and series of functions** — pointwise vs. uniform convergence, Weierstrass M-test,
   interchange with continuity / integration / differentiation, power series, radius of convergence,
   Taylor series.

Conventions:

- ID prefix: `BasicAnalysis.` (unified across the library).
- Three existing entries in the repository are **reused verbatim** at their proper positions:
  `BasicAnalysis.sec.integral` (section), `BasicAnalysis.subsec.compute` (subsection, retitled to
  fit under §4), and `BasicAnalysis.thm.NewtonLeibniz` (theorem).
- `parent` column is the id of the branch-parent in the library graph; `section` rows have empty
  parent. Row order = intended reading order.
- Shared binder variables are declared via `context` entries at the top of every `section`.
- `definition` entries carry a fully formalizable statement (no vague prose). Intuitive commentary
  lives in `remark`. Proofs are separate `proof` entries whose parent is the theorem/lemma.
